Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy describes how tf-ai-dev-exec ("the app"), a personal developer tool operated by Juntao Cheng, handles data obtained through Google APIs.

What the app accesses

When you authorize the app, it can access files in your Google Drive, limited to what is needed to upload, list, read and update development execution output files in a folder you designate.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide this function: storing and retrieving the authorizing user's own development output files. The app does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not transfer it to third parties, except as needed to provide the function or as required by law.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored only in the authorizing user's own configuration and secret stores. Files stay in the user's Google Drive. The app does not keep a separate copy of Drive content beyond temporary processing.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
